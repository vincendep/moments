Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Moments doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nobody but you can see what you keep in it, including its developer.

What Moments keeps, and where

Photos and videos

Saved to the Photos library on your iPhone, and to iCloud Photos if you use it. Moments keeps only a link to each one.

Timelines, notes and voice memos

Stored on your iPhone and synced through your private iCloud database, so your other devices and a new iPhone get them back. Only you can open it. Apple's privacy policy covers how iCloud stores it.

Places

The place you give an entry is kept with it, in the same private iCloud database. Place names and place search come from Apple Maps, so a location is sent to Apple when it is named or searched for, without anything that identifies you.

Settings

Preferences such as the timeline you last opened stay on your iPhone.

Permissions

Photos

To show your moments and save new ones to your library.

Camera

To take photos and videos for your timelines.

Microphone

To record voice memos and the sound of videos.

Location

Asked for the first time you add an entry, and only used while adding one, to note where it happened. It's optional: without it, you can still search for a place.

Moments uses them only for these features. You can change them at any time in Settings.

Crash reports

If you share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send the developer anonymous crash reports. Apple collects these, not Moments, and you can turn them off there.

Tips

Tips are taken on Ko-fi, not in the app. If you leave one, Ko-fi handles your payment and details under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting an entry or a timeline in Moments deletes it from your iPhone and from your iCloud. Its photos and videos stay in your Photos library, where you can delete them yourself.

Deleting the app doesn't delete what's in your iCloud, so your timelines come back if you install it again. To remove them for good, delete them in the app first.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about your privacy, write to support@example.com.
