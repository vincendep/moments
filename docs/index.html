Moments

A quiet frame around your own pictures and words.

Moments keeps timelines of your photos, videos, notes and voice memos on iPhone: a child growing up, a house being built, years of travel. Mark the moments that matter as milestones, and see them on one line with the time between each.

As many timelines as you need

Each grouped by month, with a scrubber to jump to any day, and every entry can keep where it happened.

Milestones

A birth, a move, a first day. See just the milestones on one line, with the time between each and the time since the first.

Only yours

No account, no ads, no tracking. Photos stay in your Photos library, and everything else in your own iCloud.

Support

Where are my photos and videos kept?

In your Photos library. Moments saves what you capture there and keeps only a link to it, so nothing is stored twice. Deleting an entry leaves its photo in Photos.

What happens when I change iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and keep iCloud on. Your timelines, notes and voice memos come back by themselves, and your photos and videos come back through iCloud Photos.

Can anyone else see my timelines?

No. Moments has no account and no server: your timelines live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, which only you can open.
